Patient rights in Virginia

A starting point for understanding protections, questions to ask, and routes to assistance.

Key protections to understand

  • Respectful, humane care and freedom from abuse, neglect, and unlawful retaliation.
  • Information about treatment, rights, restrictions, and how to raise concerns.
  • Access to applicable complaint procedures and human-rights advocacy.
  • Privacy and confidentiality protections, subject to lawful exceptions.
  • Fair review of concerns and individualized treatment decisions under applicable law.

Rights and exceptions depend on the setting, legal status, and specific circumstances. Review the official sources rather than assuming every right is absolute.

Primary Virginia sources

  • Virginia Code § 37.2-400 — rights of individuals receiving services

If you need help applying a right to a specific treatment order or hospital policy, consult the facility human-rights advocate or qualified legal counsel.
