Before submitting a concern

  1. Write down the date, time, location, and what you directly observed. Distinguish firsthand facts from information reported by others.
  2. Keep copies of relevant documents lawfully in your possession. Do not remove restricted records or share another patient's medical information.
  3. Identify the result you seek: safety measures, a rights review, preservation of records, or an independent investigation.
  4. Choose a suitable reporting channel. If you fear retaliation, ask the receiving office about confidentiality and available protections before providing sensitive details.
  5. Save a copy of your submission and any reference number or reply.
